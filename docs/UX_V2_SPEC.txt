GOAL
==================================================
Turn RevenueGuard from an engineer's investigation board into a product that a small Indian business owner and their accounts team understand in 5 seconds. Every main screen answers:
How much money am I losing? Who do I bill today? Who do I chase? What is waiting for me? Is it right, and where is the proof?

==================================================
HARD RULES (never break these)
==================================================
1. Every financial write (Zoho/Tally invoice or voucher) and every customer message goes through the EXISTING approval flow: role check, 2 different approvers above Rs 1,00,000, approval bound to payload hash + evidence_version, auto-expire when evidence changes, idempotency key, read-back verification. New surfaces (WhatsApp replies, bulk approve, phone app, Ask) call the SAME approval endpoints. Never add a second write path.
2. Agents never get write tools. No LLM can write money or send messages directly.
3. All incoming content (email, WhatsApp, PDFs, photos, voice) stays untrusted and goes through the Laya injection check.
4. Money numbers come from the deterministic validator, never from LLM text. Plain-language headlines are built from templates over validator facts.
5. Keep DEMO_MODE/mock working. Keep existing tests green (api 48, harness 40, wa-bridge 15). Add tests for everything new.
6. Indian formats everywhere: Rs sign, 4,82,000, lakh/crore, dates like 15 Oct 2026, times in IST.
7. Plain English on main screens. Words like agent, node, evidence_version, checkpoint and orchestrator appear only under Advanced.
8. Privacy: wa-bridge must drop messages from chats that are not on the allowlist BEFORE storing them (see Phase 4).

6. Alias learning: when the user links an unlinked item or corrects a customer/PO name, save the alias (e.g. "Exmpl Cust" = Example Customer, "184" = PO-184) per tenant and use it in matching.
